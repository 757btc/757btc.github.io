**Meetup October 10th 2026 - 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at [Mile Zero Marine](https://milezeromarine.com/), 115 High Street Portsmouth, VA 23704**

--- 
Ahhhh fall is in the air and its always a good time to stack some sats and understand more about how to become a sovereign individual in this digital age. Bitcoin has layers that enable faster transactions, more privacy, and ease of use. One of the most popular examples of a layer 2 on Bitcoin is Lightning. We will dive in and discuss some of the philosophy that goes into what makes a good layer 2 and some proposed layers 2's.

Even if layer 2 sounds like a complicated topic, you will want to at least swing by for the TLDR (too long did not read) and find out what it means for you. There is never a bad question, and the local Bitcoiners love to to go into good discussions to help the community understand these topics.

Come catch up with the community, hear updates on what our different groups have been working on, and help us keep the momentum going for freedom tech. See you there!
