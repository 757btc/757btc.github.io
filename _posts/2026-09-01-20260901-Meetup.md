**Meetup September 12th 2026 - 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at [Mile Zero Marine](https://milezeromarine.com/), 115 High Street Portsmouth, VA 23704**

--- 
School is back in session, but we're skipping the algebra and going straight to the good stuff!

Join us Saturday the 12th at 2:00pm for another family-friendly meetup with 757BTC crew. While the kids are getting back into the swing of the classroom, we’re hosting a masterclass of our own on Bitcoin Replay Attacks and Fork Concerns.

We'll be diving into the 'what, why, and how' of Bitcoin forks—specifically how to make sure you don't lose access to your coins when the network splits and what those mysterious replay attacks are all about. No pop quizzes here, just a relaxed environment to learn, ask questions, and chat about the sovereign stack.

Come catch up with the community, hear updates on what our different groups have been working on, and help us keep the momentum going for freedom tech. See you there!
