**Meetup August 8th 2026 - 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at [Mile Zero Marine](https://milezeromarine.com/), 115 High Street Portsmouth, VA 23704**

--- 
**Security Alert**, if you currently use a cold card to secure your Bitcoin you need to consider moving your funds to a new generated seed on a different device that is not a cold card. [https://blog.coinkite.com/coldcard-mk3-seed-generation-warning/](https://blog.coinkite.com/coldcard-mk3-seed-generation-warning/) 

Please join us at 2:00pm for a family friendly meetup with 757BTC to discuss the entropy issues with cold card and steps moving forward. More on the sovereign stack that we can all build out as a community and updates on the simulation research of Bitcoin soft forks that was presented in Wyoming last month. Come find out how the community is meeting, discussing, and using Bitcoin. There will also be some simple hands on opportunities if you are curious how it all works with a seed signer device demo (open source Bitcoin signing hardware). As always anything that improves self sovereignty and freedom tech is a worthy discussion that is always welcome. Updates on what the different meetup groups have been working on and discussion on what we have planned moving forward.
